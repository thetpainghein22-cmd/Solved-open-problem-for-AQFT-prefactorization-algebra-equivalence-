<key-original-step id="S1">
$\HK^{add,W}(M)$ is a full subcategory of $\HK^W(M)$ by C2.
</key-original-step>

<key-original-step id="S2">
Homotopical discreteness is preserved under full subcategories.
</key-original-step>

From C1 and C3,
\[
\HK^W(M) \simeq \mathcal{L}_{\widehat{W}_M}\CG(M).
\]

Hence $\mathcal{L}_{\widehat{W}_M}\CG(M)$ is homotopically discrete.

From C4, $L_{W_M}t\mathcal{P}_M$ is presented by $\mathcal{L}_{\widehat{W}_M}\CG(M)$.

<key-original-step id="S3">
Projective model structures preserve weak equivalences of operad algebras.
</key-original-step>

Thus $L_{W_M}t\mathcal{P}_M$ is homotopically discrete.

From C5, $\HK^{add,W}(M)$ is an AQFT.

Let
\[
\mathbb{A} : \mathbf{tPFA}^{add,c} \rightleftarrows \mathbf{AQFT}^{add,c} : \mathbb{F}.
\]

<key-original-step id="S4">
Right adjoints preserve limits and hence preserve $0$-truncated objects.
</key-original-step>

It suffices to show $\mathbb{F}(\HK^{add,W}(M))$ is homotopically discrete.

From C6, FQFTs are algebras over $\tau(\LBop_m)$.

From C7, the $0$-dimensional bordism category is homotopically discrete.

<key-original-step id="S5">
Algebras over a homotopically discrete operad are homotopically discrete.
</key-original-step>

Thus $\mathbf{FQFT}_m$ is homotopically discrete.

From C8, $\mathbb{F}(\HK^{add,W}(M))$ is an AQFT.

From C9, $\mathbf{AQFT}^{add,c} \simeq \mathbf{FQFT}_m$.

Therefore $\mathbb{F}(\HK^{add,W}(M))$ is homotopically discrete.

\end{proof}
